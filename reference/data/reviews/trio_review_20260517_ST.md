## Section 8: Learnings

### What Worked

- **Debutant-demotion rule validated in R5** — the only hit of the meeting was directly enabled by demoting #7 (1 start) and promoting #2 as banker. Without this rule, Strategy A matches Strategy B exactly.
- **Mode A tight pools preserved capital** — total stake $620 vs Strategy B's $1,100 (-44% cost), limiting the damage on a 1/11 session.
- **Pool coverage remained strong** — 9/11 races had ≥2 placers in the pool; the model's probability sorting was broadly correct even when bankers failed.
- **R5 market-vs-model split** — #2 ISLAND BUDDY at 37 SP (MC 25.9%) finishing 3rd was a clean MC win over market pricing.

### What Didn't Work

- **Banker reliability collapsed** — 0/11 banker wins, only 4/11 placing. The 36.4% top-3 rate is well below the season average of ~49%.
- **Going sensitivity unaddressed** — the shift to Yielding from R6 destroyed predictions. 0/6 hits on Yielding turf. #4 VERMILION TEMPEST (61.6% MC Win) ran 11th.
- **雙膽拖 structure yielded zero hits** — R4, R6, R7, R11 all used 雙膽拖 at $30/race and all missed. The cheap stakes meant low absolute damage, but the structure requires BOTH bankers to place.
- **AWT surface mismatch in R1** — simulation classified R1 as "Turf" but the actual race was on AWT. Model inputs were wrong from the first race.
- **R9 total wipeout** — all 3 placers outside the pool; MC's top-5 horses occupied positions 4th–8th+. Complete model-market disconnect.

### Strategy Adjustments

- [ ] **Add going-change gate:** if track downgrade from Good → Yielding mid-meeting, consider PASS on remaining races or switch to wider Mode C pools.
- [ ] **AWT detection:** verify surface from fixture data before running MC — R1 was AWT but simulated as Turf.
- [ ] **雙膽拖 going sensitivity:** 雙膽拖 requires extreme banker reliability; on degraded going, fall back to standard 膽拖 with wider pools.
- [ ] **Banker floor rule:** if going ≥ Yielding AND MC #1 Win% < 45%, PASS or switch to no-banker box on top 4.
- [ ] **Track Purton on low-MC horses:** R2 winner #14 LAHORE (MC 1.2%) at 8.5 SP with Purton — consider a "Purton on short-odds outsiders" saver ticket.

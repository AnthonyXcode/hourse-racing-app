## Section 8: Learnings

1. **Adj Win% banker is not always more robust than MC #1** — R5 is the clearest counterexample this meeting.
2. **Pattern A cluster** — When the frame sits entirely in **legs** but the **banker** misses (R4, R5, R9), consider a **small secondary** “no-banker” or alternate banker structure on the **widest** pools only (cost control).
3. **HV 1650 / C3** — **R7** super-dominant MC still missed **#9** in third; **Mode A** tight pools need a hard rule for **next-best Adj Place%** when field size ≥ 12.
4. **Replicate odds<10 / Rule 9** — R1’s result validates **including** short-priced outliers in the **leg** set; the failure was **banker** choice and **#7**, not **#1**’s inclusion.
5. Archive **`results_20260408_HV.json`** when scraped for downstream MC / backtest refresh.

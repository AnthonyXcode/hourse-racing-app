## Learnings

### What Worked
- **R10 pool construction was correct** — the MC identified the top 3 placers perfectly. The structural failure was in the 雙膽拖 requirement, not the probability model.
- **R7 MC overlay call was right** — #13 BLING BLING GENIUS (MC 18.7%, 29 odds) won at SP 13.0. The 422% edge call was validated.
- **Purton tracking was 100%** — every Purton mount in our pools placed (R4 2nd, R8 3rd, R10 1st).
- **R11 banker selection was correct** — PATCH OF STARS won as predicted at 6.9.

### What Didn't Work
- **Banker selection catastrophically failed** — 2/11 top 3 (18.2%) vs 52.3% season average. High-MC-confidence picks (#5 65.5%, #6 49.5%, #3 62.5%) all collapsed.
- **MC probability model was systematically wrong** — 7 of 11 races saw placers from the MC bottom half. The model showed no discriminatory power this meeting.
- **雙膽拖 structure cost real money** — R10's $582 Trio was in the pool but the dual-banker requirement prevented the hit. Both 雙膽拖 races (R8, R10) lost.
- **+Excuses flag underperformed** — 1/4 (25%) vs ~50% historical. SCMP adjustments hurt rather than helped.
- **Pool gap pattern was endemic** — 9/11 races had Pattern C (banker fail + pool gap). The model couldn't identify placers outside the top 6-7 horses by MC ranking.

### Strategy Adjustments
- [ ] **Avoid 雙膽拖 when alternative single-banker exists** — R10 proves that requiring two bankers is too restrictive. Fall back to single banker with wider legs.
- [ ] **Increase Purton weight in banker selection** — Purton was 3/3 while MC bankers were 2/11. When Purton rides a top-3 MC horse, strongly prefer that horse as banker.
- [ ] **Add a "banker fail insurance" leg** — consider expanding Mode A pools from 5 to 6 horses as default, accepting higher cost for better coverage.
- [ ] **Investigate meeting-level "chaos detection"** — 7 of 11 races were won by horses at >10x SP. This is extremely unusual. A pre-race volatility indicator could trigger wider pools or PASS decisions.
- [ ] **Review MC calibration for Turf meetings with predominantly Class 3-4 races** — this card had 8 races in C3-C4 on Turf and the model was systematically unreliable across all of them.

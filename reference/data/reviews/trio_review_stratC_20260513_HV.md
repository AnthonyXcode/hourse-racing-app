## Closing takeaways (Strategy B / stratC)

1. **Hit rate parity, worse ROI:** Matching Strategy A on hits (**33.3%**) but staking **+$100** across the card dragged ROI **lower** (**-69.6%** vs **-65.8%**).
2. **Banker dependency:** Four banker placements still yielded only three collects — **R8** showed banker-in-frame is insufficient when a wild **MC tail** (**#1**) splits **#11** and banker **#2**.
3. **“Fixable” vs “chaos” misses:** **R6** is the standout structural lesson (Pattern **A**); **R7** is the standout chaos lesson (Pattern **C**, MC ranks **8–10**).
4. **Favourites lied:** Multiple races saw short SP/MC leaders fail (**R1, R4, R7, R9** banker story) — fixed 膽拖 magnifies those failures versus adaptive sizing or alternative ticket shapes.

## 9 · Learnings

### What Worked

1. **SCMP conditional inclusion of #12 WITH ALL MY FAITH in R9 was the session's defining edge.** The +excuses bridge elevated a 0.2% MC horse into the pool — and it placed 2nd. This is exactly the scenario where the full pipeline outperforms MC-only.
2. **雙膽拖 converted for the first time in 6 campaign attempts (R7).** The 63% Adj Place threshold for #8 GLOWING PRAISES was borderline (63.9%) and it paid off. The $30 stake vs $100 for 膽拖 saved $70 on the same return.
3. **Banker selection was excellent (7/9 top 3, 77.8%).** The MC #1 horse placed in 7 of 9 races. The two failures (R3 #2, R6 #3) were both races where the pre-race review flagged specific concerns (untested distance, going change).
4. **Going change detection:** The Good to Yielding surface for R1–R4 was not modelled but the banker selections still held up (3/4 top 3 on yielding ground).

### What Didn't Work

1. **MC systematically underestimates mid-market horses that place.** Five pool-gap horses (R1#4, R2#12, R4#11, R5#12, R8#11) all had MC <2% but finished top 3 at 4.6x–16x. Market divergence accuracy was just 20%.
2. **R6 SCMP swap of #6 → #10 was wrong.** Replacing NEVER TOO SOON (28.6% Adj Place, actual 3rd) with VIVACIOUS WIN (25.6% boosted, unplaced) cost a potential Trio component. The +4 ★4 bridge was the meeting's most aggressive and it failed.
3. **R3 banker on untested 2200m distance was the session's largest error of commission.** $100 staked on NOBLE PURSUIT who had never raced the distance — flagged pre-race but not acted upon.
4. **Pool gap at $148–$577 dividends is the systematic leak.** Four races had banker + 2/3 covered but the third horse was just outside the pool. The "Adj Place ≥25%" floor is too exclusive for consistent conversion.

### Strategy Adjustments

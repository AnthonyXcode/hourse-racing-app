## Learnings

### What Worked

- **R5 SCMP draw/excuses on #8:** The +draw flag for Flying Knight's favourable barrier justified its inclusion as the 6th Mode B horse — without it, the tight pool would have missed. This is the exact SCMP edge the pipeline is designed to find.
- **R6 雙膽拖 in small field:** 4 combos at $40 returning $1,771 = the most efficient hit structure. Small-field G3s with dominant MC reads are the pipeline's sweet spot.
- **Pool coverage rate:** 5/11 races had all 3 placers inside our pool — the filtering is working. The bottleneck is banker selection and bet structure, not pool construction.
- **Pattern A detection:** Recognising that 3/11 misses were pure banker-fail with all legs correct confirms the model's ability to identify the correct place candidates.

### What Didn't Work

- **Dual-banker structure in C4:** 3 of 5 dual-banker races missed (R2, R4, R9). The 雙膽拖 saves cost but creates a binary cliff — one banker slip kills a ticket even when the pool is perfect.
- **R7 dual-banker costed $7,984:** The most expensive structural decision of the season. #6 AMAZING GAZE at 67.2% Adj Place looked solid but didn't deliver.
- **R9 SCMP -perf removing #8 ENTHUSIUM:** The -perf flag was applied for "savage attempt / stewards warning" which is a riding issue, not a fitness issue. #8 won the race. Need to distinguish rider penalties from horse performance.
- **Banker reliability:** Single-banker hit rate 33.3% is below the ~55% needed for consistent profit.

### Strategy Adjustments

- [ ] **Review dual-banker threshold:** Consider raising 雙膽拖 trigger from 63% to 68% Adj Place — would have avoided R7 dual-banker trap (#6 was 67.2%)
- [ ] **Distinguish -perf flags:** SCMP TIR "stewards/riding" penalties should NOT trigger -perf on the horse; only genuine fitness flags (couldn't keep up, laboured, tongue tie, vet note) should apply
- [ ] **R7 type salvage:** When dual-banker applies, also place a smaller single-banker ticket on MC#1 alone as insurance — cost +$60 but catches the "one banker fails" scenario
- [ ] **Track market SP < 6 pool gaps:** R10 (#9 SP 5.0) and R2 (#10 SP 5.1) were market-short exclusions. Post-SCMP, horses under SP 6 not in pool should flag as "monitor live"

## Learnings

### What Worked

- **R9 Mode A selection:** With 49.4% MC Win dominance and a clean top 5 pool, VICTORY SKY as banker delivered. The Mode A 5-horse pool exactly matched the winning combination — the system worked as designed.
- **R7 PASS call:** The wide-open classification (5 horses with diff<8) correctly flagged R7 as a pass. Result was NOTTHESILLYONE (#1) at SP 14.0 winning — pool would have had an upset-prone result regardless.
- **R2 PASS call:** HAPPY ACTION (#2, MC rank 9, 0.4%) placed 3rd at SP 15.0 — the pass was validated. Competitive R2 was too uncertain.
- **Moreira as jockey signal:** SKY VINO (rank 3, 11.8%) with Moreira won R8. The jockey boost rule should have elevated #7 toward banker consideration, potentially catching this $2,650 Trio.

### What Didn't Work

- **AWT surface banker reliability:** 4 of 7 bankers failed to place (57% failure rate). All were "dominant" MC favourites (38–50%). AWT seems to dramatically reduce MC favourite reliability — this is the 3rd AWT meeting in a row with poor banker conversion.
- **R6 model failure:** NEVER PETER OUT (50.4% MC) was the most confident call of the day and finished well below 4th. The 3 placers had combined MC probability of <0.2%. No system adjustment could have caught this.
- **R5 pool gap (#10 CALIFORNIA BAY):** A horse at 0.8% MC winning at SP 3.5 is a significant gap. Teetan's booking should have been an SCMP-level flag to at least monitor this horse. The -8 to +8 jockey boost model doesn't capture jockey-selection surprise like booking a champion jockey on a weak-form horse.
- **Pattern B dominant again (2/7):** Pool gap misses (R3, R5) remain the most common, with R3 having #12 at SP 4.7 outside the MC top 6.

### Strategy Adjustments

- [ ] **AWT banker cap rule:** When meeting is AWT-only, consider reducing Mode A to 4-horse pool (1膽+3腳) OR requiring 55%+ MC Win for Mode A — the 38–50% MC range has low conversion on AWT this season.
- [ ] **Moreira/top jockey booking flag:** When J Moreira, Z Purton, or A Badel books a rank 3–6 horse in the pool, elevate that horse as alternate banker candidate. R8 would have been a hit with #7 as banker.
- [ ] **SP < 5.0 market-short pool gap flag:** When a non-pool horse is SP < 5.0 pre-race, flag for live monitoring. R5 (#10 SP 3.5), R3 (#12 SP 4.7) were both market-flagged opportunities missed.
- [ ] **Teetan booking signal:** Teetan rode 2 upset winners today (R5, R6). A "champion jockey on surprise booking" flag might catch some of these market signals earlier.

## Lessons

1. **Best banker session in 6 meetings**: 8/11 (72.7%) banker placed — up from the dismal 44.4% at Meeting 5. The MC model's core ranking (Win% #1) was overwhelmingly correct for frame placement. The failure was pool width, not banker selection.

2. **Odds<10 rule delivered two high-value hits**: R1 #9 RAPID PHANTOM ($8.5, MC 9.3% Place → placed 3rd, enabling $297 Trio) and R6 #12 LUCKY CANDY ($5.1, MC 1.1% Place → placed 3rd, enabling $222 Trio). Both were classic "market sees what MC can't" cases. Without the rule, only R7's unprofitable $32 hit would have stood.

3. **Pattern A returned — two banker-fail races with full leg coverage**: R8 (all 3 in legs, banker 8th) and R9 (all 3 in legs including 2 odds<10 adds, banker 10th). Combined missed return estimated at $698+ (R9). This continues to be the structural ceiling of the 1-banker system.

4. **R3 missed a $1,062 Trio by 0.5% Place threshold**: #7 BIG RETURN had MC Place% 19.5% (0.5% below 20%) and odds $16 (not <10). If the threshold were ≥19.5% or "≥18%", R3 would have hit. The pure MC top 6 approach (which includes #7 as rank 5 by Win%) would have captured this.

5. **R5 missed by $0.10 on the odds<10 cutoff**: #6 LUCRATIVE EIGHT had odds exactly $10.0 — not strictly <10. It placed 3rd. If the rule were "≤10" instead of "<10", R5 would have hit (banker won, #14 placed 2nd via odds<10, #6 would be in pool).

6. **R11 staking inflation was the worst of the session**: 7 legs (3 from odds<10) produced 21 combos / $210 — the costliest bet. None of the 3 odds<10 adds placed. $150 wasted on a miss that Place%>20%-only would have incurred at just $60.

7. **Cumulative drawdown continues**: -$3,496 through 59 races (ROI -43.1%), worse than -$2,847/-41.1% after Meeting 5. However, this meeting's -54.1% ROI was better than the previous two ST meetings (-79.7%, -73.8%), suggesting some stabilisation. The hit rate of 19/59 (32.2%) remains respectable; the problem is staking cost vs dividend level.
